It's my birthday tomorrow. I'm not a fan of celebrating it for various reasons but I find it interesting that when you're younger you look forward to getting older. However, at some point, that flips and you don't really look forward to it any more, or at least I don't. 

I miss that feeling of excitement before a birthday.
